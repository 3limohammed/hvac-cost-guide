Ductless Mini-Split Installation Cost (2026)

✓ Verified Ductless Benchmarks Single-Zone: $2,300 – $4,800 Whole-Home (3–4 Zones): $7,000 – $15,000

Ductless mini-split heat pumps offer exceptional efficiency (up to 30+ SEER2) without requiring ductwork. A single-zone system costs between $2,300 and $4,800, while a whole-home multi-zone system with 3 to 4 indoor air handlers ranges from $7,000 to $15,000+.

Installation Labor & Electrical Requirements

Mini-split installations involve specialized trade work that affects total contractor billing:

  • 240V Dedicated Electrical Circuit ($350 – $900): The outdoor condensing unit requires a dedicated 20 to 40-amp circuit breaker run from the main electrical panel with an outdoor disconnect switch.
  • Refrigerant Line Set Line-Hide ($200 – $500): PVC decorative architectural gutter guards (Line-Hide) enclose the insulated copper lines, communication cables, and drain tube along the home exterior.
  • Condensate Removal Pumps ($150 – $300 per unit): If an indoor wall unit cannot drain via gravity through an exterior wall, an internal mini condensate pump is required.

Brand Tiers: Mitsubishi & Daikin vs. Pioneer & Senville

Premium Japanese manufacturers (Mitsubishi Electric, Daikin, and Fujitsu) dominate the professional trade market. They feature advanced variable-speed rotary inverter compressors that heat down to -13°F with 12-year compressor warranties. Direct-to-consumer brands (Pioneer, Senville, MrCool) cost 40% less upfront, but carry shorter warranties and may be rejected by licensed contractors unwilling to service third-party equipment.
