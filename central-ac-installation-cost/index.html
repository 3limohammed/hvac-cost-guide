Central AC Installation Cost Guide (2026)

✓ First-Time Install Benchmarks With Ducts: $4,000 – $7,800 No Ducts (With New Ductwork): $8,500 – $14,500

Installing central air conditioning costs an average of $5,600 if your home already has usable ductwork connected to a forced-air furnace. If your home has radiators, baseboard heat, or requires a complete ductwork retrofit, the total cost ranges between $8,500 and $14,500+.

Cost With Existing Ductwork vs. New Duct Installation

Home Condition Scope of Work Required Installation Timeline Total Cost Range
Existing Ductwork (Functional) Replace outdoor condenser, indoor cased coil, lineset, and disconnect. 1 Day (6–10 Hours) $4,000 – $7,800
Existing Ductwork (Needs Sealing/Modifications) New AC equipment + mastic duct sealing, new return drop, or plenum rebuild. 1 – 2 Days $5,500 – $9,400
No Existing Ductwork (Single-Story Attic Access) New AC equipment + flex ductwork and ceiling supply diffusers installed through attic. 2 – 3 Days $8,500 – $13,500
No Existing Ductwork (Multi-Story Home) New AC equipment + custom sheet metal chases cut through closets/floors + electrical panel upgrade. 3 – 5 Days $11,500 – $18,000+

Additional Costs When Adding Central AC to an Older Home

  • Electrical Service Panel Upgrade ($1,500 – $3,000): Older homes with 100-amp electrical service often lack the amperage capacity for a 30-amp AC compressor, requiring a 200-amp panel upgrade.
  • Condensate Drain Line & Float Switch ($200 – $450): Code requires a dedicated drain pipe routed to the exterior or a plumbing trap, equipped with a safety secondary float switch to prevent ceiling flooding.
  • Thermostat Low-Voltage C-Wire ($100 – $250): Upgrading from old 2-wire heat-only thermostats to modern 5-wire smart thermostats.

Engineering References

  • ACCA Manual D: Residential Duct Systems.
  • National Electrical Code (NEC Article 440: Air-Conditioning and Refrigerating Equipment).
