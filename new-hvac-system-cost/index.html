New HVAC System Cost: 2026 Budgeting Guide

✓ Budgeting Guide Average Project Range: $6,700 – $12,500 Updated: 2026

Quick Answer: The total cost of a new residential HVAC system in 2026 ranges from $6,700 to $12,500 for standard whole-home replacements (averaging $8,500). Pricing is approximately 55% equipment hardware and 45% certified labor, permits, disposal, and warranty registration.

Purchasing a new HVAC system involves far more than just buying metal boxes. A complete contractor proposal incorporates equipment, matched coils, refrigeration line sets, electrical whips, permits, and professional installation labor.

How to Negotiate and Compare Contractor Quotes

  1. Demand the AHRI Certificate: Never sign a contract that simply states "3-Ton Carrier AC." Demand the exact AHRI Certificate number verifying that the outdoor model number and indoor coil model number are tested together to achieve the promised SEER2 rating.
  2. Inquire About Labor Warranty Length: All major manufacturers provide a 10-year parts warranty. However, labor warranties are provided by the installer. Reputable companies offer 2 to 10-year labor warranties; low-ball contractors offer only 1 year (or 90 days).
  3. Confirm Permit Inclusion: If a contractor asks you to pull the permit as an "owner-builder" or claims a permit isn't necessary, find another company immediately. Unpermitted installations can jeopardize homeowner insurance claims and complicate real estate transactions.
