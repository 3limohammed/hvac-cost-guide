How This Calculation Works

Our mathematical calculation engine relies on four primary data layers:

  1. Capacity Tonnage Calculation: Uses baseline square footage bands mapped to ACCA Manual J residential load calculation standards. Standard insulation requires 1 ton of cooling capacity per 500–600 square feet in temperate climates and 400–450 square feet in hot southern climates.
  2. Equipment Cost Index: Synthesizes wholesale trade distributor price sheets across entry-level (Goodman, Payne), mid-tier (Carrier, Trane, Lennox), and premium inverter brands (Daikin, Mitsubishi, Bosch).
  3. Regional Labor Rate Index: Applies Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) Occupational Employment & Wages data (SOC 49-9021) to adjust contractor labor hours based on state and metro wage baselines.
  4. Advisory Decision Logic: Analyzes the entered age of your current system against the industry-standard $5,000 Rule (Age × Repair Cost) to advise whether repairing or replacing provides superior financial return.
