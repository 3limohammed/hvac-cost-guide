HVAC Costs Directory: 2026 Pricing Benchmarks

✓ Verified Pricing ACCA Engineering Benchmarks & BLS Labor Wage Rates Updated: 2026

Heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) projects represent one of the largest mechanical investments an American homeowner will make. The national average cost to replace a complete heating and cooling split system ranges from $6,700 to $12,500, with high-efficiency variable-speed systems exceeding $16,000.

Below is the authoritative cost comparison across all primary residential equipment categories based on standard 3-ton capacity installations (accommodating homes between 1,600 and 2,200 square feet) with existing ductwork.
