Air Conditioner Repair Cost Guide (2026)

✓ Verified Repair Benchmarks Average Repair Range: $175 – $850 Diagnostic Fee: $75 – $150

Most homeowners spend between $175 and $850 for common air conditioner repairs. However, major component failures like a failed compressor or evaporator coil replacement can reach $1,200 to $2,900.

AC Capacitor Replacement Cost: What to Expect

A failed run or start capacitor is responsible for roughly 40% of all summer air conditioning service calls. Homeowners are often surprised when a technician quotes $160 to $320 to replace a small cylinder that costs $15 to $35 at an electrical supply warehouse. Here is why the total invoice is structured this way:

  • Diagnostic Service Dispatch ($79 – $149): Covers the fully stocked service vehicle, technician drive time, and meter diagnostics (measuring microfarads ±5% tolerance).
  • Skilled Labor & High-Voltage Safety ($60 – $120): Capacitors store dangerous high-voltage electrical charges (370V–440V) even when power is disconnected. Technicians safely discharge the unit, verify proper dual-round MFD ratings, crimp insulated terminals, and verify compressor startup amperage.
  • Truck Stock & Part Warranty ($30 – $60): Professional contractors install commercial-grade capacitors (such as AmRad or Mars) with a 1-to-5-year warranty rather than cheap imported alternatives.

Cost to Fix Freon Leaks & R-410A Recharge Rates

If your AC is blowing warm air or ice is forming on the copper suction line, you likely have a refrigerant leak. Under EPA Section 608 regulations, air conditioners operate in a sealed closed-loop system; they do not “consume” refrigerant like motor oil. Merely adding refrigerant without locating the leak is an expensive, temporary stopgap.

Leak Repair Task Technical Process Typical Cost Range
Electronic Sniffer / Bubble Test Isolating leak location across service valves and flare fittings $150 – $300
Nitrogen Pressure Isolation Test Draining system, pressurizing to 350 PSI with dry nitrogen $250 – $500
Brazing Line Set Pinhole Leak Silver solder brazing on accessible copper tubing $250 – $600

Diagnostic Service Call Fees

HVAC companies charge a standard diagnostic fee ranging from $75 to $150 (or $150–$250 for after-hours emergency calls) just to send a technician to test electrical continuity, subcooling, and superheat. Reputable contractors will waive or credit this diagnostic fee toward the total cost of the repair if you approve their quote.

When to Repair vs. When to Replace

If your system requires a repair over $1,000 (such as a compressor or coil replacement) and is over 10 years old, replacement is virtually always more cost-effective. Use our dedicated Repair vs. Replace AC Decision Tool to check your exact unit.
