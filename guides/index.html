The cost figures, regional labor indices, and sizing tables provided on this website are estimates based on aggregated industry data, U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics wage reports, and ACCA engineering guidelines. Actual quotes will vary depending on ductwork condition, home thermal insulation, electrical capacity, and contractor pricing. Always obtain at least 3 written bids from licensed, insured local HVAC professionals performing Manual J load calculations before purchasing equipment.
