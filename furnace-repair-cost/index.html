Furnace Repair Cost Guide (2026)

✓ Verified Heating Benchmarks Average Repair: $150 – $650 Updated: 2026

Most common furnace repairs cost between $150 and $650. Minor fixes like replacing a dirty flame sensor or burned-out hot surface igniter are fast and inexpensive, while replacing a cracked heat exchanger can cost up to $3,100.

Furnace Component Repair Price List

Part / Repair Task Symptoms & Warning Signs Typical Installed Cost
Flame Sensor (Clean or Replace) Furnace ignites, burns for 4–7 seconds, then shuts off immediately. $100 – $260
Hot Surface Igniter (HSI) Draft inducer runs, but burners never light; glowing element cracked. $150 – $360
Thermocouple (Standing Pilot) Pilot light will not stay lit when button is released. $120 – $250
Draft Inducer Motor Loud screeching noise at startup; pressure switch error code. $380 – $890
Pressure Switch Furnace locks out before burner ignition; blocked intake pipe. $180 – $380
Gas Valve Replacement Igniter glows, but no gas enters burner manifold; odor of gas. $350 – $750
Integrated Circuit Board Furnace is completely dead; error code flashes erratic LED patterns. $350 – $750
Blower Motor Replacement Burners fire but fan never circulates air; high-limit switch trips. $450 – $1,200
Heat Exchanger Replacement Carbon monoxide alarm trips; yellow burner flame; rusted chamber. $1,400 – $3,100

Critical Carbon Monoxide Safety Warning

A cracked heat exchanger is an immediate safety emergency. It allows lethal combustion gases, including odorless carbon monoxide (CO), to mix with the breathable indoor air stream. If a certified technician diagnoses a cracked heat exchanger, they are legally required to shut off the gas valve and red-tag the unit. Because a replacement heat exchanger costs $1,400–$3,100, installing a new furnace is almost always the safer and wiser financial choice.
