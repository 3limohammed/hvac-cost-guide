HVAC Maintenance Cost Guide (2026)

✓ Maintenance Benchmarks Single Tune-Up: $75 – $195 Annual Service Plan: $150 – $320 / Year

A single seasonal HVAC tune-up typically costs between $75 and $195. Most homeowners opt for an annual service agreement (averaging $150 to $320 per year) that covers two comprehensive visits: a spring air conditioning inspection and a fall furnace tune-up.

What a Professional 21-Point Tune-Up Includes

A legitimate tune-up takes 45 to 75 minutes per system. Verify that your technician checks:

  • Electrical: Measure capacitor microfarad (MFD) capacitance against factory ratings; check contactor pitting; test compressor and fan motor amp draw under load.
  • Refrigerant: Gauge subcooling and superheat levels against factory charging tables; test for leaks if pressures are abnormal.
  • Airflow & Cleaning: Chemical foam wash of outdoor condenser coils; flush indoor condensate drain with algaecide; inspect blower wheel for dust accumulation.
  • Heating Safety: Test flue draft pressure; measure temperature rise across heat exchanger; test safety high-limit switches and flame rollout sensors.

Manufacturer Warranty Protection

Virtually all major HVAC manufacturer warranties (Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Goodman) require documented annual maintenance by a licensed contractor. If a compressor or heat exchanger fails within its 10-year warranty, manufacturers can and do demand proof of regular service before honoring replacement parts claims.
