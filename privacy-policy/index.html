Privacy Policy

✓ Policy Compliance AdSense Compliant • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Effective: September 2026

At HVAC Cost Guide (accessible from https://hvac-cost-guide.vercel.app/), protecting the privacy of our visitors is one of our primary priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by HVAC Cost Guide and how we utilize it.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, including the HVAC Cost Calculator, all calculations occur client-side in your web browser. We do not store your ZIP code, home square footage, or system age inputs in any persistent database, nor do we sell or share this information with contractors.

If you contact us directly via our Contact form, we receive your name, email address, and the contents of your message. This data is used solely to respond to your inquiry and is never shared with third parties.

2. Log Files

Like standard web hosts, HVAC Cost Guide utilizes log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information. The purpose of this information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

HVAC Cost Guide uses 'cookies' to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

4. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & AdSense Policies

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Our advertising partners include:

  • Google AdSense: Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on HVAC Cost Guide. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs.

Note that HVAC Cost Guide has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

5. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Rights

Under the CCPA, California consumers have the right to:

  • Request that a business that collects a consumer's personal data disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data that a business has collected about consumers.
  • Request that a business delete any personal data about the consumer that a business has collected.
  • Request that a business that sells a consumer's personal data, not sell the consumer's personal data. HVAC Cost Guide does NOT sell personal data.

6. Contact Information

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us through our Contact Page.
